# Presets and routines

Quick start is for the lengths you use most: a click instead of a drag. A preset starts one timer. A routine starts
several at once.

## Presets

Each button under **Quick start** in the panel is a preset. A click on one starts a timer of that length at once,
with the preset's name, color, symbol and sound.

There are eight to begin with: 5, 10, 15 and 30 minutes, and 1, 2, 3 and 4 hours. They have no names until you
give them one, as three have been given in the pictures here.

### Edit presets

Open Preferences with the sliders button at the bottom-right of the panel and choose **Presets**.

  <img class="window" src="/drag-timer/images/settings-presets.webp" width="1440" height="1256" alt="Preferences, Presets tab: eight presets in a list with drag handles, each with a colored symbol, its length and sound, and a row menu" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

Presets can be added, edited, duplicated, deleted and reordered, up to twelve of them. Drag rows to reorder, or use
the row menu. **Restore defaults…** brings back the starting set.

Each preset keeps its own:

- label and duration
- color and symbol
- sound and volume
- looping, notification and snooze choices

A preset can be as short as one second: type its duration as `45s` or `1m 30s`.

Editing a preset never changes a timer that is already running. Changes apply only to new timers.

## Routines

A routine is a named set of timers, such as Pomodoro or Morning routine, that all start together. Click a routine in
the **Routines** strip of the panel to start every timer in it at once.

The launched timers share one start time and are then ordinary timers: each can be paused, changed, cancelled or
marked done without affecting the others.

The strip appears in the panel once a routine has been made.

### Edit routines

Choose **Routines** in Preferences.

<figure class="shot">
  <img class="window" src="/drag-timer/images/settings-routines.webp" width="1440" height="1256" alt="Preferences, Routines tab: two routines, Pomodoro with 2 timers and Morning routine with 3 timers" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

Routines can be added, edited, duplicated, deleted and reordered. Inside a routine, add a custom five-minute timer or
copy any Quick start preset in, then edit and reorder its timers.

A routine holds snapshots, not links:

- Changing a preset later does not change the copy of it inside a routine.
- Changing a routine does not affect timers that are already running.

Like presets, a routine timer can be as short as one second.
